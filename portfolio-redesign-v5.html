Who I Am

I'm a mobile and full-stack engineer, currently building at ShareCar (CodeReset) — where I rebuilt the core booking system for production scale and shipped an end-to-end KYC identity verification flow on a webhook-based architecture.

My work runs from React Native clients through Django/DRF and FastAPI backends down to schema design in Supabase and PostgreSQL. I like the problems that sit between layers: session race conditions, WebView context loss, OTP providers that need swapping without touching the client.

Previously an AI & Data Intern at Deloitte Nigeria. Graduating First Class (4.85/5.0) in Computer Science from Pan-Atlantic University in June 2026.
